# Checklist ingestion design cover note

The design is documentation-only. It reconciles the active PF-M5.1 catalog
boundary, current X1/C1/X8b schema, configuration applicability proposal, and
all 43 supplied sample files. No migration, application code, deployment or
database environment changed.

## Decisions requested from Trent

1. Approve two checklist layers: an organization-owned approved version and a
   separately platform-published canonical version.
2. Choose the capability mapping for source submission, organization approval,
   private proposal, platform review and platform publication.
3. Decide whether an organization may confirm a private subject link or only
   propose it for platform resolution.
4. Approve a neutral subject-kind vocabulary and the treatment of teams/groups.
5. Approve a release-scoped base card identity above the current exact variant.
6. Choose or defer multi-subject revenue/margin attribution. Recommended safe
   default is no attribution.
7. Confirm that unresolved subjects and unknown configuration applicability do
   not block organization checklist approval or basic intake.
8. Confirm Panini 2025 Basketball Prizm as the first end-to-end slice, with
   Court Kings fixtures for cross-product and multi-subject acceptance.

The central correction is that organization approval and platform publication
are different actions over different records. Import remains catalog-only and
creates no inventory, cost, asking price, reservation or listing.
