## Universal operator-lifecycle acceptance overlay

Every PF-C checkpoint that adds or materially changes an operator interaction must
also satisfy `../OPERATOR_LIFECYCLE.md` and `../UX_WORKFLOW_CONTRACT.md`:

- **W1 Entry:** entry points, parent object, origin, preconditions.
- **W2 Commit:** exact business mutation or handoff and visible saved result.
- **W3 Continue:** state-aware primary and secondary next actions.
- **W4 Review/leave:** review or correction plus Back, Cancel, and safe exit.
- **W5 Repeat/resume:** repetitive path, progress, incomplete exit, durable resume.
- **W6 Recover:** empty, denied, duplicate, stale, conflict, and failure behavior.
- **W7 Return:** route, parent object, filters, selection, unsaved state, and focus.

These are PA/CR workflow obligations, not new database invariants and not authority
to implement a dependent checkpoint early. Screenshots prove render; behavioral
scenarios prove W1–W7.
