## 1. Verdict

**Backend staging readiness: CONDITIONAL ACCEPT.**

The foundation is real and mostly holds under attack: no cross-tenant read or write was found in any batch; the client-callable surface is closed by default with explicit grants, pinned search paths and RLS on every new table; idempotency keys are per organization and detect changed replays; reporting readers aggregate over the full authorized cohort before paginating and bind their cursors to actor, organization, filters and revisions; the X8 seams are inert and closed. All 159 migrations replay cleanly and every database-backed CI step passes in an independent environment.

Acceptance is conditional because four claims that the acceptance record and the brief state as facts are false for specific writers, and each is reachable by an ordinary authenticated client through documented or still-granted RPCs:

1. "Sensitive writers recheck mutable authority after waiting on locks" is not true for `e10_org_lot_reserve`, `e10_org_receive_po_line`, `e10_org_reverse_receipt`, or for any X6a–X6d customer writer including transaction posting (IMPL-2, IMPL-3).
2. Inventory conservation is broken by the still-granted single-line receipt reverser after a quarantine disposition: phantom stock that can be reserved and sold (IMPL-1).
3. "Break activity is not finalized customer spend" and "a native sale and a later import do not count twice" are not enforced: a released board sale can be posted as official spend, and a native sale plus its import both post (IMPL-4, IMPL-6).
4. "Native provenance cannot be forged through ordinary imports" holds for commercial events but not for market observations (IMPL-5).

Conditions for unconditional acceptance (all are small, local corrections):
- Fix IMPL-1 through IMPL-6 and IMPL-8 (dual-membership catalog visibility), with the two-connection revocation tests that are currently missing for X4b/X4d/X6 writers (EVID-2).
- Gate `e10_org_list_commercial_comments` (IMPL-7) before any Track B screen exposes it.
- Correct the acceptance trace rows listed in EVID-3 and commit the nine local scope/checklist documents the acceptance record cites (EVID-1), or remove them as audited sources.

Production readiness: not assessed and not claimed; the no-cutover boundary (no `e10` schema in production) was not independently verified because this review did not contact production. UI readiness: not applicable; no Track B screen exists, and the identity tables have no client writer at all (SCOPE-1).

## 3. Implementation defects

Severity: high = wrong stock or money, or an authorization claim that fails; medium = integrity or authorization inconsistency with bounded impact; low = contract or robustness.

### IMPL-1 (high) Single-line receipt reversal leaves phantom on-hand stock after a quarantine disposition
- Requirement: inventory conservation; `docs/TA_X4_REMAINDER_PLAN.md` "Reversal removes each line's effective accepted quantity, including quantity accepted by later quarantine decisions".
- Where: `public.e10_org_reverse_receipt(p_org,p_receipt_id,p_reason,p_idempotency_key)`, X3c wrapper `20260911185500_e10_ta_x3c_purchase_order_lifecycle.sql:240-262` delegating to `_e10_org_reverse_receipt_x4d` (`20260910204500_e10_ta_x4d_receipt_posting.sql:197-226`). The X4g corrective (`20260912000365`) made only the batch reverser disposition-aware. The single-line reverser subtracts `stock_receipt_lines.accepted_quantity`, which dispositions never update, and it is still `GRANT EXECUTE ... TO authenticated` although it is absent from the integration handoff.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/inventory_x4/scenA.sql`): receive 0 accepted / 5 quarantined against a PO line; `e10_org_review_receipt_disposition(...,'accept',3,...)` gives item qty 3, lot accepted 3 / quarantined 2; `e10_org_reverse_receipt(...)` returns ok/reversed. Afterwards: item qty still 3, lot status `reversed` with accepted 3, movement ledger sum 3 with no offsetting movement, reversal row quantity 5 with null movement id, PO open commitment back to 10. The 3 phantom units were then reserved through `e10_org_inv_reserve` and can be sold, and the PO line can be received again in full. Control: `e10_org_reverse_receipt_batch` on the identical state removes 5 and leaves 0.
- Correction: make `e10_org_reverse_receipt` delegate to the X4g-wrapped batch reverser, or revoke it from `authenticated`. Add a regression test: disposition, then single-line reverse.

### IMPL-2 (high) Purchasing and inventory writers commit after a lock wait even when authority was revoked during the wait
- Requirement: brief section 5 "Sensitive writers recheck mutable authority after waiting on locks"; trace row "Post-lock authority/reference recheck".
- Where: `e10_org_lot_reserve` wrapper (`20260912000365_e10_ta_x4g_receipt_disposition.sql:138-153`) rechecks only after the advisory lock, then `_e10_org_lot_reserve_x4b` (`20260910194500:110-160`) takes the lot and item row locks and writes reservations and movements with no further check. `e10_org_receive_po_line` and `e10_org_reverse_receipt` wrappers (`20260911185500:210-262`) recheck before calling `_x4d` bodies that take fresh item/receipt row locks (`20260910204500:107-111,157-160,205,212`) and write `e10_inventory_movements` directly, bypassing the capability check inside `e10_org_emit_inventory_movement`. `e10_org_lot_consume`/`e10_org_lot_release` (`20260910201500:250-345`) check once on entry; their post-lock safety today rests only on the legacy `e10_org_emit_inventory_movement` helper happening to check the same capability, and neither checks organization status.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/authz_x2_x4/race_stale_auth.sh`, `race_receipt_stale_auth.sh`): session 1 holds the inventory item (or receipt) row `for update`; session 2, an authenticated holder of the capability, calls the writer and is observed waiting (`wait_event_type = Lock`); session 3 commits `allowed=false` on the capability, or suspends the membership; session 1 releases. Session 2 then commits: `e10_org_receive_po_line` posts a receipt (receipts 1, item qty 3, movements 1); `e10_org_reverse_receipt` reverses; `e10_org_lot_reserve` creates an active reservation after both a capability revocation and a membership suspension. Control: `e10_org_lot_reserve_for_demand` (X4h) under the same interleaving returns `42501 reserve_inventory_denied`, and the X3c/X3d/X4e/X4f/X4g writers recheck correctly (verified by reading and by the existing X3c race tests). With the organization set to `suspended`, `e10_org_lot_consume` and `e10_org_lot_release` still succeed.
- Correction: move the authority reread (org active, membership, capability, and `can_receive_at` for receipt writers) after the last blocking lock in `_e10_org_lot_reserve_x4b`, `_e10_org_receive_po_line_x4d`, `_e10_org_reverse_receipt_x4d`, `e10_org_lot_consume` and `e10_org_lot_release`, or have the wrappers take every row lock before their recheck as `e10_org_receive_batch` does. Add revocation races to the X4b/X4c/X4d concurrent tests (none exist today).

### IMPL-3 (high) Customer transaction writers never recheck authority after lock waits
- Requirement: same as IMPL-2; posting is explicitly a review priority.
- Where: `e10_org_post_customer_transaction_draft` checks `has_org_cap` at `20260911001500_e10_ta_x6c_customer_transaction_posting.sql:329`, waits at `:331-350` (`for update` on the draft and advisory locks per activity and source), writes at `:351-357` with no recheck. Same shape in approve (`:291` then `:293-298`), amend (`:276` then `:279-281`), reopen (`:314` then `:316-318`), create (`:263` then `:266`), `e10_org_adjust_customer_transaction` (`20260911003000:146` then `:154-157`), `e10_org_finalize_customer_transaction_component` (`20260911004500:106` then `:110-113`), `e10_org_attribute_customer_activity` (`20260911000000:187` then `:191`), `e10_org_create_customer` (`:102` then `:105`) and `e10_org_record_customer_activity` (`20260910234500:79` then `:91`). X6e/X6f/X6g, X7b, X8 and F4 writers do recheck.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/customer_x6_x7b/race_post_revoke.js`): session S holds the draft row `for update`; the authenticated poster blocks (Lock wait observed); S deletes the `act.post_customer_transactions` grant and sets the membership to `suspended`, commits. The post then completes `{"ok":true,"status":"posted"}` with one posted transaction while the membership is `suspended`.
- Correction: re-run `e10.is_org_member`/`e10.has_org_cap` and the org-active check immediately after the last lock in each X6a–X6d writer, as X6e–X6g do. Add a lock-wait revocation case to `ta_x6c_customer_transaction_posting_concurrent_test.js`, which today covers only replay and one-effect races.

### IMPL-4 (high) A released native break sale still counts as provisional activity and can be posted as official spend
- Requirement: brief section 3; X6g plan "A board sale is provisional customer activity, never official spend"; refunds, releases and corrections must not become spend.
- Where: `e10_org_release_native_break_sale` (`20260911014500_e10_ta_x6g_native_break_sales.sql:166-190`) appends a `released` transition but leaves the sale's activity observation unchanged; `e10_org_customer_provisional_activity` (`20260911031500_e10_ta_x7b_spend_reporting.sql:553-606`) excludes only posted or linked activities and never consults the transitions; `e10.insert_customer_draft_revision` (`20260911001500:225-233`) and posting (`:336-343`) require only that the native activity exists with the matching customer.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/customer_x6_x7b/e2.sql`): commit sale 1 (30) on a slot, release it, commit sale 2 (35) on the same slot. Provisional activity reports `activity_count 2, known_provisional_merchandise_subtotal 65`. A draft with a `native` line for the released sale approves and posts, making the released sale official spend.
- Correction: require a current native sale in draft validation and again at post under the activity lock; exclude or label released sales in the provisional projection. `ta_x6g_native_break_sale_test.js` never reads provisional activity or attempts to post a released sale.

### IMPL-5 (medium) Ordinary intake can label market observations as `native`
- Requirement: brief section 4 and handoff "Native-system provenance is reserved for trusted native writers".
- Where: `_e10_org_stage_intake_x5b` (`20260910214000_e10_ta_x5b1_stable_intake_parsing.sql:51-54`) accepts any `p_source_kind` permitted by the table check (`manual,csv,native,api`); `_e10_org_commit_intake_x5d` copies it into `e10_market_observations`. Commercial events enforce the rule (`normalize_commercial_event_envelope`; v2 rejects `native`); market observations do not.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/intake_x5/t1_dedup.sql`, case G1): an authenticated holder of `act.manage_intake` stages `('native','inventory-ledger','forged-ref',...)` with a completed sale of 999; resolve and commit succeed; `e10_current_market_observations` holds `native|inventory-ledger|forged-ref|999`. The X7d2 helpers `e10.market_source_universe` and `market_provenance_matches` filter on `source_kind`, so the row enters the native evidence universe. Tenant-local impact.
- Correction: reject `p_source_kind='native'` in `e10_org_stage_intake` and `e10_org_stage_corrected_intake` unless the caller is `service_role` (the pattern in `e10_org_open_customer_transaction_reconciliation`), or remove `native` from the client intake check.

### IMPL-6 (medium) A native board sale and an imported record of the same sale can both be posted
- Requirement: brief section 3 "so a native sale and a later import do not count twice"; X6g plan "the sale contributes once".
- Where: deduplication keys are `(capture_source, source_connection_id, source_line_id)` only (`20260911001500:99`, `20260911010000:43-76`); the native identity `('native','live-break:<session>','sale:<id>')` is never correlated with an import's identity in `insert_customer_draft_revision` or at post; nothing forces a slot-bearing import through reconciliation.
- Reproduced (`e2.sql`): post native sale 2 (35); record an `import` activity for the same slot and post it through an ordinary draft; the customer summary shows `known_official_subtotal 100` (30 released + 35 native + 35 import), `line_count 3, order_count 3`. The documented reconciliation path (open, then link) does dedupe; the ordinary path does not stop the duplicate.
- Correction: refuse a posted line whose `(break_session_id, break_slot_id)` already carries a posted line for a current sale unless it comes from a reconciliation decision, or require slot-bearing import activities to be linked rather than posted; otherwise document the limitation plainly.

### IMPL-7 (medium) Any organization member can read internal purchasing comments on documents they cannot see
- Requirement: X3a `20260910174308:170` "Comments remain server-only until an audience-safe workflow projection is added"; supplier invoices are server-only pending financial read authority.
- Where: `e10_org_list_commercial_comments` (`20260911213745_e10_ta_x3e_commercial_comments.sql:297-315`) requires only membership and returns `body` and `created_by` for both audiences.
- Reproduced (`kit/counterexamples/authz_x2_x4/authz_probe.sql`, R01/R02): a member with zero capabilities gets `42501` on `select from e10_supplier_invoices` but receives `{"body":"INTERNAL: supplier overcharged us, unit cost really 4.10","audience":"internal"}` from the comment list. Cross-tenant isolation of the function is correct.

### IMPL-8 (medium) Members of more than one organization cannot read the shared catalog through RLS
- Requirement: handoff "Never infer the first membership"; trace "Multi-membership: implemented".
- Where: `20260910171106_e10_ta_x1_identity_foundation.sql:196-203`: SELECT policies on `e10_catalog_releases`, `e10_catalog_variants`, `e10_catalog_variant_subjects`, `e10_catalog_identity_mappings` use `e10.is_platform_admin() or e10.current_org() is not null`; `e10.current_org()` (`20260717120000:163`) is NULL unless the user has exactly one active membership. The legacy `e10_players/teams/sets/cards` policies inherit the same predicate.
- Reproduced: a user with one active membership sees `e10_players` rows; a user with active memberships in org0 and a second org sees 0 rows on `e10_players` and the X1 catalog tables while `e10.is_org_member(org0)` is true and the RPC readers (which use `exists(membership)`) still work.
- Correction: replace the predicate with `exists (select 1 from e10_organization_memberships m where m.user_id = auth.uid() and m.status = 'active')`; add a dual-membership case to `ta_x1_identity_foundation_test.sql`.

### IMPL-9 (medium) Receipt reversal ignores location authority
- Requirement: handoff "Location authority is rechecked inside the applicable writer"; PUR-01. Disposition (`20260912000365:249`) and batch receiving (`20260912000335:181`) require `e10.can_receive_at(destination)`; reversal, which removes stock at that location, does not.
- Where: `e10_org_reverse_receipt_batch` (`20260912000350:171-173,261-265`) and `e10_org_reverse_receipt` (`20260911185500:250-258`, `_x4d:179-181`) check org, membership and `act.resolve_recovery` only.
- Reproduced (`authz_probe2.sql`, P02–P04): a user holding every purchasing and receiving capability but no location grant (`can_receive_at` false) is denied receiving and disposition at location A, yet reverses both a batch receipt and a single-line receipt at location A (item qty 0).
- Correction: require `e10.can_receive_at(p_org, receipt.destination_location_id)` in the post-lock guard of both reversers, or state in the handoff that reversal is deliberately location-agnostic.

### IMPL-10 (medium) X7a, X7b and X7c organization RPCs ignore organization status
- Requirement: handoff "Organization-scoped client RPCs require ... an active organization and membership".
- Where: `e10.is_org_member`/`e10.has_org_cap` check only membership status. X3c–X4h, X7d, X7e and X8 add an explicit `e10_organizations.status='active'` check; the X7a readers and the coverage writer `e10_org_review_attendance_coverage` (`20260911023000:198-244`), the X7c readers (`20260911043000`) and `e10_org_review_provider_presence_normalization_policy` (`20260911033000`), the X7b spend readers, and `e10_org_purchase_destinations`, `e10_org_list_commercial_comments`, `e10_org_vendor_comment_projection` do not.

### IMPL-11 (medium) Same-source re-imports create duplicate current observations
- Requirement: matrix "Idempotency and fingerprints prevent duplicate effects"; acceptance case "Import replay/correction".
- Where: `e10_org_stage_intake` (`20260910224500_e10_ta_x5f_atomic_correction_import.sql:62-76`) treats a batch as a replay or supersession only when `(source_kind, source_connection_id, source_reference)` are byte-identical and durable; the stable `source_event_id` guard exists only in `e10_org_commit_corrected_intake` (`:137-145`), not in `e10_org_commit_intake`; the X5a fingerprint uniqueness was dropped in X5a.1 and re-added only as a partial index for durable sources.
- Reproduced (`t1_dedup.sql`): (a) `('csv','vendor','daily.csv',...,'sha-v1')` then `('csv','vendor','daily-2.csv',...,'sha-v2')` with the same `source_event_id` both commit as separate current observations; (b) an identical manual batch (same fingerprint) under a new idempotency key creates a second batch and observation; (c) an identical csv file with a NULL connection duplicates; (d) `'vendor '`, `'Daily.csv'` and `''` variants each start a new lineage. Only a reviewed X7d equivalence decision can later collapse them; the screener otherwise counts them separately.
- Correction: apply the stable-source-event guard in the ordinary commit path; normalise connection and reference before lookup; decide explicitly whether identical-fingerprint manual and connection-less files are replays, and document that same-source deduplication is otherwise a reviewed step.

### IMPL-12 (medium) "Immutable configuration versions" and "append-only provider mappings" are not enforced by the database
- Requirement: matrix X1 row; handoff "Rejection does not mutate players or provider mappings".
- Where: `e10_product_configuration_versions` (`20260910171106:46-72`) and `e10_catalog_identity_mappings` (`:128-165`) have no `before update/delete` trigger, unlike every X1b/F4/X5 evidence table; the mapping revision model (`..._current_uq where is_current`) actually requires updating the prior row before a new revision can be inserted.
- Reproduced (as `service_role`, `kit/counterexamples/identity_x1_f4/`): `update e10_product_configuration_versions set base_units_per_package=1, packaging_kind='case', barcode='CHANGED', state='retired'` on a version referenced by inventory: UPDATE 1; `update e10_catalog_identity_mappings set match_status='rejected', mapping_revision=5, source_payload='{"rewritten":true}'`: UPDATE 1; unreferenced versions delete.
- Impact: fourteen commercial tables reference `configuration_version_id`; rewriting a version in place changes what every historical line means. No client writer exists (SCOPE-1), so the exposure is operational, not a client attack.
- Correction: triggers that allow only `state` transitions on versions and only the `is_current` flip on mappings, or reword the documents to "not client-writable".

### IMPL-13 (medium) Quarantine-only receipts from the single-line receiver cannot be reversed by the documented batch reverser
- Where: `_e10_org_receive_po_line_x4d` (`20260910204500:129-140`) writes no receipt commercial event when accepted is 0 (X4e does); `_e10_org_reverse_receipt_batch_x4f` (`20260912000350:165-171`) raises `receipt_evidence_missing` for such lines.
- Reproduced (`scenB.sql`): receive 0/0/4 quarantined via `e10_org_receive_po_line`; `e10_org_reverse_receipt_batch` fails with `receipt_evidence_missing` (also after a damage-only disposition); the legacy `e10_org_reverse_receipt` reverses it but records no correction event.
- Correction: emit the receipt-ledger evidence event for zero-accepted lines in the X4d path and backfill, or let the X4f lookup synthesise the origin event as `capture_native_inventory_event` does for pre-X5c movements.

### IMPL-14 (low) Contract and robustness items (all reproduced)
- Customer draft lines accept an activity in another currency: `insert_customer_draft_revision` (`20260911001500:225-232`) never compares the activity's currency with the header; a CAD activity (50) posted under a USD draft enters the USD cohort.
- `e10_org_customer_spend_summary` (v1) returns zeroed `full_cohort_totals` on an empty trailing page (`20260911031500:340-376`); with no `has_more`, clients paging to exhaustion always see it. The v2 grid is correct.
- Draft line validation lets missing/NaN/Infinity amounts fall through to raw `23502`/`23514`; a draft referencing the same activity twice is accepted and approved and fails only at post with `23505`.
- Typed dispatcher: `e10.x8_assert_args` (`20260912000381:27`) validates integers with a digits-only regex and no magnitude bound and requires uuid keys only to be strings; `"cursor":"not-a-uuid"`, `"supplier_id":"not-a-uuid"` return `22P02` and `"limit":99999999999999999999` returns `22003` instead of the documented `22023`. No third-party data is exposed.
- X4 writers: second active reservation for the same (lot, session) or (lot, demand reference) raises raw `23505`; duplicate `lot_code` inside one batch raises raw `23505`; a batch receipt reusing a key first used by `e10_org_receive_po_line` raises raw `23505` instead of `idempotency_key_mismatch`; reserve with `5` then `5.0` under one key fails as a mismatch (fingerprint uses `p_quantity::text`).
- v1 `e10_org_record_commercial_event` (`20260910213000:155-156`) hashes `p_occurred_at::text`, so an exact replay under a different session time zone fails with `idempotency_key_mismatch`; v2 hashes the epoch; v1 is still granted to authenticated.
- Cross-organization existence oracle: client-supplied line ids are checked without `organization_id` (`20260911203110:225,309`) or inserted into globally unique keys (`20260911185500:334,589-619`, `20260911200637:152,165,304,318`), so a foreign id is distinguishable from a random one (raw `23505`, or `financial_document_line_identity_immutable`). No content is disclosed.
- `e10_org_vendor_comment_projection` returns `document_status` and `document_reference` for invoices and credits to members without financial authority; `e10_org_supplier_workspace` returns receipt `line_ids` to any member. No amounts leak.

## 5. Scope disagreements and design notes (not defects of the accepted slice)

- SCOPE-1: No RPC writer exists for any X1 identity table (`e10_product_masters`, `e10_product_configurations`, `e10_product_configuration_versions`, `e10_catalog_releases`, `e10_catalog_variants`, `e10_catalog_variant_subjects`, `e10_catalog_identity_mappings`, `e10_unique_items`). Every identity row in every test is inserted as `postgres`. Since "direct table writes are not an integration contract", there is currently no supported way for an organization to create a product, version, release, variant or physical copy. The handoff should say so; Track B purchasing screens cannot ship without it.
- SCOPE-2: `e10_org_set_location_financial_access` gates customer financial reporting only; no X2–X4 purchasing cost field is location-gated. Purchasing cost visibility is organization-level (`financial.actual_cost.read`, `act.view_financial_estimates`). If the owner expects location-scoped hiding of purchasing costs, that is unimplemented, not broken.
- SCOPE-3: Prepare/approve/post and X8b draft/approve/commit separation is capability-only; one user holding all capabilities completes the chain alone, and a draft creator may approve their own draft. No document requires maker-checker separation; state it if intended.
- SCOPE-4: Legacy inventory RPCs (`e10_org_inv_consume`, `e10_org_inv_edit_item`) move on-hand quantity without touching lots; lot writers bound availability by `min(lot accepted minus committed, item qty minus legacy active)`, so no over-commit was reachable, but `e10_inventory_lots.accepted_quantity` is never decremented by legacy consumption and lot-level reads (X7e lifecycle/exposure) can overstate lot availability. A break session mixing legacy and lot reservations on one item cannot consume through the legacy API (`lot_linked_reservation_requires_lot_writer` aborts the whole call).
- SCOPE-5: Fractional quantities are accepted by every X4 writer on `each`/card configurations (reserve 2.5, consume 0.25, receive 0.5). Arithmetic conservation holds; unit granularity is not enforced.
- SCOPE-6: Actual-cost history treats a receiver-typed `actual_unit_cost` as actual even with no source document; consumers treating it as "last paid" should know it is operator-asserted. `reviewed_import` evidence quality and the `inventory-ledger` connection id are likewise client-assertable on imported commercial events.
- SCOPE-7: Pre-existing (Foundation A1, outside X1–X8): the squashed baseline grants `ALL` on several legacy tables (`e10_break_events`, `e10_break_sessions`, `e10_cards`, `e10_members`, `e10_workspace`, the `e10_obs_*` family and others) to `anon` and `authenticated`, including TRUNCATE, which RLS does not govern. RLS policies and the absence of `is_platform_admin` execute for `anon` block reads in practice (the A7 gate documents this), and PostgREST cannot issue TRUNCATE, but a future direct-SQL surface would be exposed. Not part of the reviewed expansion.
- SCOPE-8: The reviewed work sits in one open PR (#5) titled for gate A6-0, carrying 297 commits and 389 files against `main`, with sixteen migrations renamed after staging applied them (so `supabase db push` refuses). This is a process observation: reviewability of the eventual merge is poor, and the staging ledger cannot be reproduced by the CLI from the repository without manual ledger edits.

## 6. Optional enhancements
- Namespace derived idempotency keys (`corrected:`, `corrected-commit:`) separately from user keys, which share `unique(organization_id,idempotency_key)`; a colliding user key currently makes a later corrected commit fail (own org only, fully rolled back).
- Guard transitions out of `committed` on `e10_intake_batches` (service role can flip a committed batch back to `validated`, after which its rows become editable; re-commit is still blocked by the commits uniqueness).
- Bind `p_limit` into the X7e signed cursors as the market cursors do (harmless today).
- Source `total_count` in the X7e readers from the ranked set rather than the page window (unreachable edge today).
- Add CHECK constraints so copy-level keys (`serial_numerator`, `grade`) cannot appear in `e10_catalog_variants.attrs`, and consider serial uniqueness per (org, variant) and serial ≤ print run.
- Escape LIKE metacharacters in `e10_org_find_customers`.

## 7. Verified as sound (what the counterexamples could not break)
- Cross-tenant isolation: more than 100 probes as a hostile org-B admin with every capability across X2–X8 (foreign PO, supplier, location, configuration, invoice, credit, allocation, comment, lot, reservation, receipt, disposition, intake batch/row/observation/event, customer, draft, transaction, reconciliation case, query context, action draft, outbox consumer) all returned `42501`/`22023`/`23514` with no mutation. Every object lookup carries `organization_id = p_org` and child tables use composite `(organization_id, id)` foreign keys, so a lookup bug could not link rows across tenants. Org-scoped RLS on the org-owned X1 tables works for dual members.
- Direct-API surface: `anon` cannot execute any `public.e10%` function; `authenticated` cannot execute any `_x4b/_x4d/_x4f/_x3d1*` body, any `e10.*` helper beyond the predicate functions, the v1 dispatcher, the X7c runner or the outbox claim/ack; all SECURITY DEFINER functions in `public`/`e10` pin `search_path`; RLS is enabled on every table created by X1–X8 and on every table in `public`; the new tables carry no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grants to clients and most no SELECT; the born-locked probe passes.
- Idempotency: keys are per organization everywhere; changed payload under a reused key fails with `idempotency_key_mismatch`; retry after a failed attempt works because nothing persisted; JSON key order is canonicalised; CAS revisions return `40001`.
- Inventory: no over-commit was reachable under the seven X4 two-connection race suites (real Lock waits, post-wait rereads) or under manual races; consume beyond reserved, double release, reversal after consumption, reversal of a reversed receipt, disposition after reversal, over-receipt within and across writers, negative/zero/NULL/NaN quantities, and expected-allocation misuse are all refused; invoice-only and source-less receipts fabricate no PO or allocation rows; the batch reverser removes the effective quantity after dispositions; lock orders are consistent across writers and use no `SKIP LOCKED` in X4.
- Correction lineage: append-only and non-forking (second supersession `55000`, cycles `22023`, self-reference blocked, replacement must be a committed re-import); originals untouched; UPDATE/DELETE on observations, supersessions and commits rejected even for `service_role`; corrected import is atomic (partial failure leaves zero rows).
- Commercial events: `native` and `system` provenance refused for ordinary callers in v1 and v2, including after setting the receipt GUC in-session; native movement capture is unique per movement.
- Customers and spend: draft state machine (amend-while-approved, stale revision, replay with different revision, posted not reopenable); adjustments bounded by cumulative refunds, currency, sign and known components; unknown stays NULL with `*_known=false` and `complete_official_total` NULL; USD and CAD cohorts separate; merge/split/attribution effective without money change; location-scoped financial readers see exactly their lines with no counts for hidden lines; the X7f grid's four sorts are stable with UUID tie-break, totals identical on every page and equal to v1 and to contributions, aggregate filters applied before pagination, cursors rejected on filter/sort/actor/org change or tampering, 100,001-line refusal exercised.
- Reporting and seams: screener, drill-down, lifecycle, valuation and attendance readers aggregate over the full cohort before paging (verified with fixtures larger than the page); market cursors bound to actor/org/endpoint/filters/sort/limit/source universe/revisions; X7e HMAC cursors reject tampering (`22023`), other actor or revision change (`40001`), other org; secrets unreadable by clients; X8a contexts bound to user and org, refused after revoke/expiry/membership removal, capacity `54000`; dispatcher output key set equals the direct RPC and every delegate re-authorizes; X8b drafts create no business row until commit, require exact revision, detect referenced-row drift after locks (`40001 action_references_stale`), and the ordinary writer's authority (including destination `can_receive_at`) applies; X8c is service-only, single-owner leases with generation, stale-token refusal, ack replay, no external dispatch mechanism anywhere; shared catalog facets writable only by platform admins, org overrides visible only to their org, decisions append-only.
- Identity: composite FKs block cross-org copy/variant links; same-name players supported after the F4 index change with no dependent code path; X1b and F4 writers are platform-admin only, bounded, append-only, idempotent, CAS-protected and recheck authority after locks.
- Handoff accuracy: all 72 documented signatures (66 explicit plus the two "each" groups) match `pg_get_function_identity_arguments` exactly; the capability tables match the function bodies for 61 documented functions.
